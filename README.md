# 🍕 Pizza Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Pizza Sales Analysis Dashboard** is a data analytics project designed to analyze pizza sales performance and identify key business trends.

The project uses **SQL and Power BI** to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights. The analysis focuses on revenue, orders, pizza quantity, customer ordering patterns, pizza categories, pizza sizes, and product-level performance.

The final output is an interactive **Power BI dashboard** that helps understand sales performance and identify the best- and worst-performing pizza products.

---


## 📌 Problem Statement

The pizza business generates a large volume of sales data, but raw transactional data alone does not provide a clear view of overall sales performance.

The objective of this project is to analyze pizza sales data and identify important business patterns related to **revenue, orders, quantity sold, pizza categories, pizza sizes, and individual product performance**.

Using **SQL and Power BI**, the project transforms raw sales data into meaningful KPIs and interactive visualizations to answer key business questions such as:

* What is the total revenue and total number of orders?
* What is the average order value?
* Which pizza categories and sizes contribute the most to sales?
* Which pizzas generate the highest revenue?
* Which pizzas have the highest quantity sold and total orders?
* Which pizzas are underperforming?
* How do order volumes change over time?

The final Power BI dashboard provides a centralized view of these metrics and helps stakeholders understand sales performance and make data-driven business decisions.


## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Analyze overall pizza sales performance
* Track key sales KPIs
* Identify daily and monthly order trends
* Analyze sales contribution by pizza category
* Analyze sales contribution by pizza size
* Identify top-performing pizzas
* Identify bottom-performing pizzas
* Compare pizza performance based on revenue, quantity, and total orders
* Provide business-oriented insights through an interactive dashboard

## 🔎 Key Insights

### 🏆 Best Sellers

* **Thai Chicken Pizza** generated the highest revenue.
* **Classic Deluxe Pizza** generated the highest total quantity sold.
* **Classic Deluxe Pizza** also generated the highest number of total orders.
* Large-size pizzas contributed the maximum sales.

### 📉 Worst Sellers

* **Brie Carre Pizza** generated the lowest revenue.
* **Brie Carre Pizza** had the lowest total quantity sold.
* **Brie Carre Pizza** also had the lowest number of total orders.

### 📈 Product Performance

The dashboard provides Top 5 and Bottom 5 pizza rankings based on:

* Revenue
* Quantity sold
* Total orders

This helps identify high-performing products and products requiring further business attention.
